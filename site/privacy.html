Privacy

This website collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no tracking. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs (IP address, time, page requested) to serve and protect the service; Lightmorphic does not receive them. The app-launcher menu in the header loads one script from apps.lightmorphic.com, a Lightmorphic server; it sets no cookies, stores nothing, and sends nothing about you.

The app

The Lightmorphic Text app stores your snippets only on your own computer. Its only network connections are to GitHub, to check for and download updates of the app itself and of Espanso, and to Debian's public archive when a missing clipboard tool needs fetching. No usage data is collected or sent anywhere.
