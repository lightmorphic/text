Complaints

If something about this website or the Lightmorphic Text app is wrong, unfair or broken and you want it dealt with, email complaints@lightmorphic.com. Say what happened and, if you can, when. You will get a reply from a person, and a straight answer about what will be done.

Bug reports are welcome on the issue tracker too, if you prefer working in the open.
