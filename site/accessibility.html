Accessibility

This website was checked against the WCAG 2.2 AA rules on 7 September 2026, by reviewing the built pages: colour contrast on every text and background pair, keyboard access with visible focus outlines, a skip link, image descriptions, heading order, page language and zoom left unblocked. Nothing failing was left in.

Honest limits: an automated and read-through review finds roughly half of real barriers. This site has not yet been tested with a screen reader or a switch device, and the app itself aims for the same standard but has had the same style of review, not a specialist audit.
